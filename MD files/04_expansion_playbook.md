## What Changes When You Expand to EU

### Tax — The Main Complexity
**OSS (One Stop Shop) VAT:**
- If you sell to EU customers above €10,000/year combined across all EU countries, you must register for OSS
- OSS is one registration (in any EU country — Italy makes sense for you) that covers all EU countries
- You collect local VAT rate from EU buyers and remit quarterly via the OSS portal
- This is not as scary as it sounds — software like **Quaderno** or **TaxJar** calculates and files it automatically
- Cost: Quaderno ~€50/month when you need it. Not needed until you hit the threshold.

**UK → EU customs:**
- Post-Brexit, goods shipped from UK to EU customers are exports — may incur import VAT/duties for the buyer
- Solution: use EU-based suppliers (Spocket, Ankorstore, local wholesalers) for EU orders — goods shipped within EU = no customs
- CJ Dropshipping has EU warehouses (Germany, Spain) — prioritise these for EU orders

### What Stays the Same
- Shopify store works for EU — just add EU languages and currencies (Shopify Markets feature, free)
- Product catalog is the same — no need to rebuild
- Brand stays the same — just translate listings

## Italy-Specific Setup

### Why Italy First
- Owner speaks fluent Italian — huge advantage for customer service, supplier negotiation, social media
- Italy is the 3rd largest EU e-commerce market
- Less saturated than Germany/France in most niches
- TikTok is extremely popular in Italy — strong organic marketing potential

### Sales Channels in Italy
| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| Amazon.it | Separate marketplace from Amazon UK — new seller account or link existing |
| eBay.it | Same eBay account, add Italy as shipping destination |
| Shopify store | Add Italian language + EUR currency via Shopify Markets |
| Subito.it | Italian classifieds — useful for testing demand, not for scaling |
| TikTok Shop IT | Not yet launched — watch for announcement, be ready to be early |
| Etsy | Already EU-accessible from your UK shop |

### Amazon.it Specifically
- You can add Amazon.it to your existing UK seller account via "Build International Listings"
- This auto-translates and lists your UK products on Italian marketplace
- Pricing can be auto-adjusted by currency and fees
- **Important:** reviews don't transfer — you start from zero on .it

### Italian Customer Expectations
- Delivery time: Italians expect 3–5 days for domestic orders. Chinese suppliers (10–20 days) will generate complaints.
- Use EU warehouse suppliers for Italian orders — CJ EU warehouse or local Italian 3PL
- Customer service in Italian — this is your advantage. Use it. Respond in Italian.
- Returns: Italian consumer law gives 14-day return right (EU standard) — factor this into your policy

### Italian Social Media for Marketing
- TikTok Italy: very active, younger demographic, high engagement
- Instagram Italy: strong for lifestyle and beauty products
- Facebook Groups: Italian consumer groups are very active — organic marketing possible
- Influencer marketing: Italian micro-influencers (10k–100k followers) are often affordable

## EU Expansion Beyond Italy

### Market Priority Order
1. **Germany** — largest EU e-commerce market, high spending power. Amazon.de is dominant.
2. **France** — strong market, Amazon.fr + local players (Cdiscount, Fnac)
3. **Spain** — growing fast, less saturated than DE/FR

### Amazon Pan-European Programme
When you're ready to scale EU seriously:
- Amazon's Pan-European FBA lets you store inventory in one EU country and Amazon distributes it across EU
- One listing, automatically translated and localised
- Requires EU VAT registration (OSS covers this)
- This is the most scalable EU route — plan for it but don't set it up until you're profitable in UK

### EU-Specific Supplier Advantages
- **Ankorstore:** European brands, ships within EU, no customs issues
- **Faire EU:** Same model
- **Spocket:** Strong EU supplier network
- **BigBuy:** Spain-based dropship wholesaler, ships across EU, has Shopify integration

## Technical Changes Needed for EU Expansion

### Shopify
- Enable Shopify Markets (free) — adds currency conversion and language switching
- Install a translation app — **Weglot** (paid, ~€15/month) or **Langify** for Italian translation
- Add EUR as a currency — Shopify Payments handles conversion automatically
- Update shipping zones to include EU countries

### Cloudflare Trend Radar
- Already built with multi-country support (UK/IT/DE/FR) — just activate the Italian and EU tabs
- Amazon.it and Amazon.de scrapers already planned in the build
- Google Trends already supports country filtering — no changes needed

### Supplier Engine
- Add EU warehouse filter — already planned
- Add Italian-language outreach email template
- Prioritise suppliers with EU warehouses for EU orders
